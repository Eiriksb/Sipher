# Sipher 0.2.0

Captions for talking NPCs.

## New

- **Captions for NPCs and other entities.** Server mods can now show captions for any entity, for example villagers that talk back in [They Will Talk](https://github.com/Eiriksb/they-will-talk). They appear as a bubble above the entity and as a line in the transcript, translated into your reading language just like other players' captions.
- The transcript shows the name of whoever is speaking, player or not.

## For mod developers

- New server-side API: `io.github.eiriksb.sipher.api.SipherCaptions.show(entity, line, partial, language, text, english, listeners)`. It only sends to players who have Sipher, and like `PlayerCaptionEvent` it stays compatible within a major version.

## Compatibility

- Clients and servers on 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 can play together. A 0.1.0 client shows NPC captions in the transcript only, without a bubble.
- No changes to language packs: installed packs keep working and nothing new needs downloading.

Feedback and bug reports: https://github.com/Eiriksb/Sipher/issues (please include *Copy diagnostics*)
